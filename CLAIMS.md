Several sessions rewrite catalog routes into Ren'Py at once. **Claim a route here before you start
it** (plan, art or script), and update the row when its status changes. One route per branch and
worktree (`catalog-renpy-<route>`); the shared files (`game/catalog/`, `screens.rpy`) change from
one session at a time. Session names are the ones `ListAgents` shows. Times are US Eastern.
Message the session that holds a claim (`SendMessage`) before you touch its route, and drop a
claim you won't do by deleting its row.

Status words: **plan** (drafted, awaiting sign-off), **signed off**, **script** (being written),
**built** (script done, walks pass, art pending or partial), **art** (pictures generating),
**merged** (on main).
