## What stays from the book

- **The smallest man in the gym wants to get big.** His training partner, futa and enormous, mixes his post-workout shakes. He's been drinking them for three months without asking what's in them.
- **Act one runs on rationalisations:**
  - the smooth chest is shaving for definition;
  - the softness is water weight;
  - the tenderness is pec growth.
- **The reveal, ch. 5.** He finds a row of shaker bottles labelled with his name, and it's her, in them. The coach explains: she did this to herself years ago, alone. She can't say "I want you", so she engineered it instead; it was cowardice, not conquest. **So she stops.** She hands him the bottle and won't touch him.
- **The stall, ch. 6:** eleven days, and his body humiliates him.
- **The terms, ch. 7.** He negotiates terms: one bottle every other day, two weeks, no touching. On the fourth night he asks her to break them.
- **Straight from the source, ch. 8:** the steepest jump.
- **The ending, ch. 9-10.** The change completes in the gym in front of the regulars. At closing she finally says the sentence, badly. **He got big. Just not the way he meant.**
- **The chastity cage** comes in ch. 4. The coach narrates every change with cheerful clinical delight.

His body gives in and he ends up begging.
